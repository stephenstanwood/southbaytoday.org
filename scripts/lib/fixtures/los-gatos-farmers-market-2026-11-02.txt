About the Los Gatos Farmers' Market Held in Downtown Los Gatos at the Town Park Plaza on Main Street & N. Santa Cruz Avenue, the Los Gatos Certified Farmers’ Market offers seasonal fruits and vegetables from Santa Clara County year-round. With over 40 local farmers and food producers, the market features Asian vegetables, honey, mushrooms, eggs, fresh roasted herbed-crusted chicken and more. Market size 40 + farmers and vendors Time Every Sunday, 9:00 am to 1:00 pm Location Downtown Los Gatos Town Park Plaza on Main Street & N. Santa Cruz Avenue
